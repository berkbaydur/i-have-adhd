§01 Services

One senior engineer, five roles. You use whichever ones the job needs.

01Architecture & technical consultancy

System design reviews, target architectures and the decisions in between: build or buy, monolith or services, which cloud, which protocol. You get written recommendations your team can act on, with the reasoning behind each one.

Architecture reviewsTech due diligenceADRsRoadmaps
02IoT systems, end to end

Connected products from the device up: provisioning, telemetry pipelines, OTA updates, fleet management and the dashboards on top. I work on the layer where hardware and software teams usually talk past each other.

MQTTBLEEdge gatewaysOTAFleet ops
03Hands-on software delivery

When you need code more than advice, I write it: backend services, APIs, integrations and the infrastructure they run on. I leave behind tests, docs and a codebase your own team can own.

Backend & APIsCloud infrastructureCI/CDIntegrations
04Interim team leadership

I step in as engineering lead while you hire, reorganise or recover a delivery that has slipped. That covers running the team day to day, setting up processes that fit its size, and handing over cleanly once a permanent lead is in place.

Engineering managementDelivery processHiringMentoring
05Technology evaluation & adoption

Before you commit to a platform, vendor or tool, I trial it against your real constraints, write up what I found and, if it holds up, roll it out. That saves your team from finding the problems six months in.

§03 Engagements

Billed by the hour. Judged by what ships.

  1. Intro call

    Thirty minutes, free. We work out whether I'm the right person for it, and if not, who might be.

  2. Scope & rate

    A short written scope, an hourly rate and an estimate of hours. No retainers unless you want one.

  3. Work in the open

    I work in your tools and your repos, inside your team's rhythm. Hours are logged against tasks so the invoice holds no surprises.

  4. Hand over

    Documentation, recorded walkthroughs and a team that no longer needs me. That's how I measure a good engagement.
